abstract: 'This paper proposes a new method for the classification 
problem of turbojet aircraft, prop aircraft and helicopter under 
the condition of low-resolution radar system. Based on the jet 
engine modulation (JEM) characteristics of the aircrafts’ 
radar echoes, we use the Empirical Mode Decomposition 
(EMD) algorithm to extract the three-dimensional microDoppler feature vector. Experimental results based on the 
simulated training data and the measured test data are 
presented, including the performance analysis for different 
Signal-Noise-Ratio (SNR) cases and comparison with some 
related methods.'
