abstract: 'For robust classification of moving wheeled and 
tracked vehicles using returned micro-Doppler signals within 
short dwell time, the influence of receiver white noise and low 
spectrum resolution are encountered. In this paper, noise 
reduction and super-resolution are realized simultaneously via a 
redundant dictionary based l1 -norm optimization method. 
Experiments based on the measured data are presented, 
including the analysis of noise reduction performance, and the 
evaluation of classification robustness for different signal-tonoise ratio cases. The experimental results are also compared 
with related methods. '
