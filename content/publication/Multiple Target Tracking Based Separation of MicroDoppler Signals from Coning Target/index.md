abstract: 'Micro-Doppler (m-D) signature based target 
classification methods have received the intensive attention from 
the radar automatic target recognition (RATR) community. Since 
the m-D signals of a coning target with micro-motion are always 
multicomponent, it is important to separate these m-D frequency 
components for feature extraction and parameter estimation. This 
paper develops a separation algorithm based on multiple target 
tracking (MTT) technology, where the m-D frequency curves are 
assumed to be the tracks of maneuvering targets. The proposed 
method shows the good performance on the electromagnetic 
computation data in the experiments. '
