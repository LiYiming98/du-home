abstract: "This paper presents an adaptive detector based on maximum a posteriori (MAP) Rao test specially for the sub-Gaussian symmetric alpha-stable (SGSαS) sea clutter background. Since SGSαS distribution does not have a closed-form probability density function (PDF) expression, there are few researches on the problem of target detection in SGSαS clutter background. In this paper, firstly the closed-form PDF expression of the SGSαS distribution is given in terms of the Fox's H-function. Then the MAP Rao-SGSαS detector in alpha-stable clutter is designed based on two-step MAP Rao test. Experimental results based on the simulated data verify the effectiveness of the proposed detector."
