abstract: 'In this letter, we introduce a novel noise-robust modi-
fication method for Gaussian-based models to enhance the performance of radar high-resolution range profile (HRRP) recognition
under the test condition of low signal-to-noise ratio (SNR), and
we develop an efficient scheme for its computation. This noiserobust modification method is implemented by revising the trained
Gaussian-based model according to the estimated SNR of test
HRRP. We apply the proposed method to adaptive Gaussian
classifier and truncated stick-breaking hidden Markov model.
Experimental results demonstrate that the proposed method can
significantly improve the average recognition rate for noisy HRRP
test samples while offering recognition performance comparable
to that of original model for clean HRRP test samples. Moreover,
even when the SNR of test HRRP samples is not precisely estimated, we can still obtain an acceptable result with the proposed
method.'
